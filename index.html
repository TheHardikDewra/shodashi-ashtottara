The Sri Shodashi Ashtottara Shatanama Stotram is a sacred 108-name hymn to Goddess Shodashi (Tripura Sundari), preserved in the Pūrvakhaṇḍa of the Brahmayāmala Tantra. It is a dialogue in which Lord Brahmā reveals the 108 names to the sage Bhṛgu, declaring them to be extracted from the thousand names of Sundarī - guhyād guhyataraṃ guhyaṃ - secret, more secret than the secret, the very heart of secrecy. This is the same stotra you hear playing in Lalita Maa Tripura Sundari's world inside the Tantra Sadhana app.
